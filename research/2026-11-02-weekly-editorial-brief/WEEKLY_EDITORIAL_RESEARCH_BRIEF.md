## 3) Top-3 Commissioning Outlines

## A) AI Infrastructure's New Constraint

Working headline:
- AI's Next Bottleneck: Capital Is Available, Capacity Is Scarce

Spine statements:
1. The market's central AI question has shifted from whether demand exists to where physical and organizational constraints bind first.
2. In this phase, return distribution depends less on narrative leadership and more on execution across foundry throughput, tool availability, and capex-to-cash conversion discipline.
3. The valuation risk is no longer demand collapse; it is timeline slippage in the infrastructure stack.

Section structure:
1. Opening thesis and the regime shift in the AI cycle
2. Structural map of the stack: hyperscalers, GPU vendors, foundries, lithography, power
3. Incentive analysis: who can monetize scarcity vs who absorbs capex drag
4. Capital implications: concentration, dispersion, and scenario probabilities
5. Long-term interpretation: from software narrative to industrial economics

Required chart set:
- NVIDIA top-line/data-center scale reference
- TSMC Q3 actuals and Q4 guidance table
- ASML Q3 figures and full-year range framing

Key uncertainty flags to include:
- geopolitical/external policy shocks
- timeline risk in manufacturing transition points
- demand concentration in a limited buyer set

3. Cross-jurisdiction comparison matters because transmission channels differ even when the inflation problem appears similar.
