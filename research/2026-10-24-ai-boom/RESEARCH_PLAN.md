# Article 1 Research Plan

Project: SwissCapital.news
Article focus: The AI Boom: Economic Revolution or the Next Great Market Mispricing?
Date: 2026-10-24
Status: Stage 1 planning
Owner: Editorial Desk

## 1) Scope and format decision

Selected format: Analytical Essay
Target range: 1,500-2,000 words
Why this format:
- topic requires cross-domain analysis (technology, earnings, valuations, macro)
- evidence base can support scenario analysis without requiring full strategic-deep-dive length

## 2) Draft thesis candidates (to validate with evidence)

Thesis A:
AI value creation is real but uneven; market pricing is currently discounting a narrower and faster earnings conversion than most adopters can deliver.

Thesis B:
The AI cycle is transitioning from software narrative to infrastructure economics, where power costs, financing conditions, and depreciation discipline determine who captures durable returns.

Thesis C:
AI can still justify elevated valuations in selected firms, but index-level outcomes depend on earnings breadth beyond a small group of mega-cap infrastructure providers.

## 3) Core research questions

1. What portion of current AI-linked capex is already linked to observable revenue and margin contribution?
2. How quickly can AI capex translate into free cash flow after depreciation, energy, and financing costs?
3. Which sectors outside technology show measurable AI productivity gains versus narrative adoption?
4. Under what macro conditions (rates, inflation, energy prices) do current AI valuations remain defensible?
5. What indicators would signal overinvestment versus sustainable commercialization?

## 4) Evidence map (required source classes)

A. Macroeconomic and productivity context
- IMF World Economic Outlook
- OECD productivity and digital economy datasets
- BIS and World Bank productivity/capital reports

B. Monetary policy and rates
- Federal Reserve statements and SEP
- ECB macro projections and policy communication
- SNB policy assessments

C. AI infrastructure and corporate capex
- Earnings calls, 10-K/20-F, investor decks from Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Nvidia, AMD, TSMC, ASML, selected utilities
- Data center power and electricity reports (IEA, EIA, grid operators where relevant)

D. Market valuation and earnings breadth
- Index concentration and earnings contribution data (S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, STOXX Europe 600 where applicable)
- Company-level FCF conversion and capex intensity trends

E. Counterevidence and constraints
- Slower monetization indicators
- competition-induced margin pressure
- depreciation and financing-cost pressure
- delayed enterprise adoption evidence

## 5) Scenario framework (1-5 year horizon)

Bull case:
- assumptions: productivity acceleration broadens, AI revenue conversion improves, cost per token declines, rates remain manageable
- market implication: earnings breadth expands, valuation premium remains elevated but more distributed

Base case:
- assumptions: clear value creation but uneven implementation quality, selective sector winners, moderate margin gains
- market implication: dispersion remains high; stock picking matters more than index beta

Bear case:
- assumptions: capex outruns monetization, high depreciation and energy burden, pricing pressure compresses returns
- market implication: multiple contraction in crowded AI winners, relative resilience in cash-flow defensive sectors

## 6) Data integrity protocol

For each material numerical claim capture:
- claim text
- source organization
- publication date
- direct URL
- data cutoff date
- classification: historical data / forecast / analyst estimate / management guidance
- independent cross-check source
- confidence level: high / medium / low

Rules:
- if source date is unclear, claim cannot be promoted to final draft
- if cross-check conflicts materially, conflict must be disclosed in article text
- if a number cannot be verified, omit it
