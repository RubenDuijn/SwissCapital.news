# AI Boom Article Upgrade: Audit and Research Plan

## Existing Article Audit

1. Strengths
- Clear core thesis: AI can be economically transformative while still vulnerable to valuation mispricing.
- Strong institutional tone aligned with SwissCapital.news standards.
- Useful macro framing connecting rates, inflation, and energy.

2. Weaknesses
- Limited quantified market context on yields, real yields, index structure, and credit conditions.
- Scenario section present but too high-level for portfolio monitoring.
- Needed clearer separation of quarterly metrics, annual metrics, and forecast assumptions.
- Needed explicit falsification conditions for the central thesis.

3. Editorial Risks Identified
- Potential reader over-interpretation of "AI growth" as equivalent to "equity upside".
- Some references were broad portals; stronger use of directly measurable figures was required.

## Research Plan and Cutoff

1. Research cutoff
- Date: 2026-10-24
- Market and macro observations used through latest available official series/filings at revision time.

2. Primary-source priorities
- Microsoft FY26 Q4 press release and annual figures.
- NVIDIA latest quarterly investor release page.
- Federal Reserve SEP (September 2026).
- EIA STEO (October 2026 release).
- FRED market series for rates, breakevens, Brent, and index levels.

3. Method
- Include only figures that could be directly verified from source pages or official public data series.
- Keep periods comparable (quarterly vs annual clearly labeled).
- Add scenario framework with assumptions, observable indicators, implications, and invalidation triggers.

4. Known limitations
- IMF WEO/GFSR pages returned access restrictions during this pass from tooling context. They are retained as institutional references, but no unverified numerical claims were attributed to them.
