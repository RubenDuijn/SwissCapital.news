# Geopolitics and Markets Article Upgrade: Audit and Research Plan

## Existing Article Audit

1. Strengths
- Correct core framing: geopolitical relevance depends on economic transmission, not headlines.
- Good institutional tone and policy-awareness.
- Useful initial scenario structure.

2. Weaknesses
- Needed more dated market evidence (rates, spreads, FX, energy) tied to specific observation windows.
- Needed clearer distinction between observed moves and interpretation.
- Needed tighter regional differentiation (Europe, Switzerland, US, exporters, vulnerable importers).
- Needed stronger threshold-style indicator framework for investors.

3. Editorial Risks Identified
- Over-attribution risk: political events and market moves can coincide without causality.
- Excessive use of broad portals rather than specific measurable inputs.

## Research Plan and Cutoff

1. Research cutoff
- Date: 2026-10-24
- Market observations included only where officially accessible and date-stamped.

2. Primary-source priorities
- EIA STEO (dated energy assumptions).
- Federal Reserve SEP.
- SNB policy communication.
- USTR and BIS policy frameworks.
- FRED series for Brent, yields, real yields, breakevens, high-yield spreads, and key FX rates.

3. Method
- Keep market claims narrow, dated, and verifiable.
- Distinguish observation from explanation in each key market section.
- Build 6-18 month scenarios with explicit assumptions, indicators, implications, invalidation rules.

4. Known limitations
- Some desired regional equity and commodity series were not consistently available through the same direct source pipeline in this pass; analysis prioritizes high-confidence series over breadth.
- IMF WEO/GFSR access limits in tooling context prevented direct extraction of dated numeric details; no unverified IMF figures used.
