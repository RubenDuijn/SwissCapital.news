# Article 2 Research Plan

Project: SwissCapital.news
Article focus: Geopolitics and the Markets: How Conflict, Energy and Trade Are Reshaping the Economic Outlook
Date: 2026-10-24
Status: Stage 1 planning
Owner: Editorial Desk

## 1) Scope and format decision

Selected format: Analytical Essay
Target range: 1,500-2,000 words
Why this format:
- topic requires explicit transmission channels from geopolitical events to inflation, rates, earnings, and asset pricing
- needs scenario horizon (6-18 months) with uncertainty and confirmation indicators

## 2) Draft thesis candidates (to validate with evidence)

Thesis A:
Recent geopolitical shocks matter for markets primarily when they alter energy availability, shipping reliability, and inflation persistence, not when they remain localized headlines.

Thesis B:
US-China strategic competition is increasing policy-driven cost friction in technology and trade, creating a structurally higher floor for supply-chain risk premia.

Thesis C:
Europe remains disproportionately exposed to imported energy volatility and external trade disruptions, making growth and earnings outcomes more policy-sensitive than in prior cycles.

## 3) Core research questions

1. Which current geopolitical channels have measurable macro transmission to inflation and growth?
2. How much of current oil/gas price behavior reflects physical disruption risk versus precautionary risk premium?
3. How are US-China restrictions and tariffs changing capital expenditure location and supply-chain redundancy costs?
4. How exposed are European earnings and bond yields to renewed energy and shipping shocks?
5. Which indicators distinguish short-lived market volatility from durable regime change?

## 4) Evidence map (required source classes)

A. Energy and shipping
- EIA STEO and tables
- IEA electricity/oil updates where accessible
- Official statements from key institutions on flow constraints and inventories

B. Monetary and inflation channels
- Federal Reserve statements and SEP
- ECB monetary policy statements
- SNB policy assessment and inflation path

C. Trade and policy fragmentation
- USTR section 301 and tariff process documentation
- BIS export control guidance and relevant policy updates
- official EU/US policy communications where extractable

D. Market and earnings transmission
- corporate filings for energy-sensitive and capex-heavy sectors
- equity concentration and sector performance context
- credit spread and rates sensitivity context from official and institutional data

## 5) Scenario framework (6-18 month horizon)

Bull case:
- assumptions: geopolitical stress contained, energy and shipping normalize, inflation decelerates, rates ease in line with guidance
- market implication: risk appetite improves, cyclicals and non-megacap breadth improves

Base case:
- assumptions: episodic disruptions continue but do not trigger sustained physical shortages; inflation stays above target but manageable
- market implication: range-bound indices, higher cross-sector dispersion, continued quality premium

Bear case:
- assumptions: renewed energy shock plus persistent inflation delays easing cycle and raises financing stress
- market implication: equity multiple compression, tighter credit conditions, defensive leadership

## 6) Data integrity protocol

For each material claim capture:
- claim text
- source organization
- publication date
- direct URL
- data cutoff date
- classification: historical data / forecast / estimate / policy statement
- independent cross-check source
- confidence level

Rules:
- no unverifiable figures in final draft
- conflicting figures disclosed explicitly
- sensitivity to date and revision status documented
