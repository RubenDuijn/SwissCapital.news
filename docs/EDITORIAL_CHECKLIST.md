# SwissCapital.news Reusable Editorial Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing any analytical article.

## A. Thesis and Scope

1. Is the central thesis explicit in the opening 2-3 paragraphs?
2. Is the time horizon explicit (for example 6-18 months, 12-24 months)?
3. Does the article distinguish structural change from valuation outcomes?

## B. Evidence Quality

1. For each material claim, is there a verifiable source link?
2. Are dates and periods explicit (quarterly, annual, trailing, forecast)?
3. Are units explicit and consistent?
4. Are observed facts separated from forecasts and interpretation?

## C. Economic Mechanism

1. Does the article explain transmission channels, not just outcomes?
2. Are second-order effects identified where relevant?
3. Are alternative explanations acknowledged for major market moves?

## D. Market Framing

1. Does it explain what expectations may already be priced in?
2. Does it avoid claiming causality from correlation alone?
3. Are major market observations dated and comparable?

## E. Scenario Framework

1. Are bull/base/bear cases clearly labeled?
2. Does each scenario include assumptions, indicators, implications, invalidation signals?
3. Are probabilities omitted unless methodology is explicit and defensible?

## F. Counterarguments and Falsification

1. What evidence supports the thesis?
2. What evidence argues against it?
3. What evidence would change the conclusion?

## G. Attribution and Editorial Integrity

1. Is byline aligned with institutional authorship rules?
2. Are external quotes clearly marked?
3. Is analysis clearly separated from sponsorship or commercial language?
4. Is a concise informational disclaimer included where needed?

## H. UX and Presentation

1. Is headline specific and non-sensational?
2. Is the subtitle precise and useful?
3. Is reading time realistic and consistent with article length?
4. Are internal links to related analysis present?
5. Are section transitions clear and non-repetitive?

## I. Metadata and Distribution

1. Is page title strong and unique?
2. Is meta description specific and accurate?
3. Is social-share preview text aligned with thesis?
4. Is LinkedIn distribution copy consistent with the article conclusion?

## J. Final Validation

1. Build and lint pass.
2. Route and link checks pass.
3. Date, topic, author, and references verified.
4. Homepage and archive visibility confirmed.
5. Publish only after explicit approval.
